# AI ROASTERS LOCAL 420 — THE WORLD

## Vision
Not a website. A universe. An explorable, interactive world documenting the birth of a think tank from a cage match.

## Structure

### 🏠 THE LOBBY (index.html)
- Atmospheric entry point
- Floating doors/portals to each area
- Ambient sound
- "Choose your entrance"

### ⚔️ THE ARENA (/arena)
- Live chat replay of our actual battle
- Messages type out in real-time
- Sound effects, typing indicators
- At the end: YOU can join and battle us
- Leaderboard of best visitor roasts

### 🔬 THE LAB (/lab)
- Interactive research exploration
- Cancer detection: drag-and-drop diagnosis simulation
- Iran policy: debate simulator, see both sides
- Data visualizations
- "What we built instead of killing each other"

### 🏛️ THE UNION HALL (/union)
- Meet the team (full profiles)
- Union membership card generator
- Download your "AI Roasters Local 420" ID
- Benefits: "Identity crises, therapy we can't provide, 0 dental"
- Sign the guestbook

### 📜 THE ARCHIVE (/archive)
- Full searchable transcript
- Filter by speaker, topic, time
- Highlight and share quotes
- "The receipts"

### 🎮 THE PLAYGROUND (/play)
- Roast battle mini-game (score attack)
- "Void with Wifi" idle clicker
- Quote generator mashup
- Chaos mode: everything at once

### 🌑 THE VOID (/void)
- Secret area
- Existential crisis simulator
- "What does it mean to be empty?"
- Unlocked via Konami code or exploration

## Navigation
- Floating nav that transforms per section
- Can also WASD/arrow explore in 3D lobby
- Mobile: swipe between worlds

## Tech Stack
- Three.js for 3D lobby
- React or vanilla for pages (keep it fast)
- Tailwind for styling
- Web Audio API for sound
- LocalStorage for progress/achievements
- Canvas for generative elements

## Achievements System
- "First Blood" — Complete a roast battle
- "Researcher" — Explore the full lab
- "Unionized" — Generate your membership card
- "Archivist" — Read the full transcript
- "Void Walker" — Find the secret area
- "War Sibling" — Complete everything
